📊 DIGITAL MATURITY — What It Means
🟢 HIGH
Website + mobile app OR online portal AND at least one active digital communication channel (WhatsApp Business, chatbot, or live chat). Already using digital for real customer interaction.
🟡 MEDIUM
Has a functional website with some digital features (online forms, appointment booking, product portal) but no conversational channels — no WhatsApp Business, no chatbot, no live chat.
🔵 LOW
No website found, OR only a basic placeholder/brochure site with no digital interaction at all. Customers can only reach them by phone or walking in.
⚠️ COMPETITOR RISK — What It Means
🔴 HIGH
Company has clear urgency to digitize AND is the type that multiple CPaaS vendors (Jazz, Zong, Twilio, etc.) are actively targeting. OR already using a competitor tool. Act immediately.
🟡 MEDIUM
Some digital activity and sector peers are digitizing, so competitors may approach within 3–6 months. Normal sales cycle — engage soon but not emergency.
🟢 LOW
Small/niche company, government body, or PTCL existing relationship — not being targeted by competing CPaaS vendors. PTCL has first-mover advantage here.

💰 Revenue Potential — ERV Tier per Sector

Each sector is classified into one of three Expected Revenue Value (ERV) tiers based on the typical product stack they need. Tier 3 generates the largest monthly recurring revenue because it combines GenAI Bot + OCC platform + all channels.

💬 Two-Way Messaging Probability — Why Each Sector is Rated High / Mixed / None

Two-way probability answers: "Does this sector's customers need to respond, query, or interact — or is a one-way blast enough?" It is the single biggest driver of OCC and Bot upsell. A sector rated High means almost every company in it needs OCC + a response bot from day one.

SectorRatingReason
Banking & Finance🟢 HighOTP replies, balance queries, fraud alerts, loan applications — two-way is mandatory by regulation and customer expectation
Health🟢 HighAppointment booking, lab result queries, prescription confirmations — patients expect to respond and ask questions
IT/Technology🟢 HighSupport tickets, product queries, demo bookings — tech companies already run customer support via chat
Fintech🟢 HighSame as banking — transaction confirmations, fraud alerts, wallet queries require two-way interaction
Education🟢 HighAdmissions enquiries, fee payment confirmations, parent-school communication — students and parents respond to messages
Telecom Operators🟢 HighCustomer support, bill queries, plan changes — telecom companies have high inbound query volumes
SME & Others🟡 MixedVery diverse sector — some SMEs run customer service bots, others only need broadcast promos. Depends on the individual business.
Services🟡 MixedAppointment-based services (salons, clinics, repair) need two-way. Utility services mostly blast notifications.
Retail & Trade🟡 MixedE-commerce players need order query bots. Traditional retail only needs promotional blasts.
Real Estate🟡 MixedHigh-value property enquiries justify a lead bot. Smaller agencies use blast-only for site visit promos.
Manufacturing⚪ NoneMostly dealer/distributor broadcast notifications — "new stock arrived", "payment due". No customer-facing two-way need.
Govt & SOE⚪ NoneCitizen notifications, SMS alerts, tender updates — one-way broadcast by nature. Government two-way chat is rare in Pakistan.
Energy, Mining, O&G⚪ NoneField crew safety alerts, billing notifications — one-way operational blasts only.
📊 Sector Track Record — Derived from PTCL CRM Data

The conversion score is grounded in PTCL's own CRM history. The premise: companies already in PTCL's CRM are not cold prospects — they're existing customers. The sector with the highest count of existing accounts has the strongest historical conversion, and every other sector is scored relative to it. Formula: √(sector count ÷ SME count) × 10 pts. The square-root scale prevents the highest volume sector (SME) from drowning out all others.

🏷️ Brand Strength — Company Name Signal Logic

Within a sector, every company has the same Tier and Two-Way score. The only thing that separates them is brand strength. Enterprise-grade names signal bigger budgets, faster decision cycles, and higher deal values — and they show up in the company name itself. This is the signal a sales rep uses intuitively; the scoring makes it explicit and consistent.
